## Test scene cast (Chapters 1 and 2)

### Tobious, 23, farmer and sluice repairer
- Dark curly hair, long enough to tie back [bible, visual bible]. He cuts it short in Veyr [8].
- Weather-darkened skin, working hands, strong but not trained to fight [bible].
- Clothes: damp undyed tunic, cloth belt with a fold that holds bread [1, 3]. The visual bible adds an ochre mantle over one shoulder, a rope belt and leather sandals.
- Scene marks: a swollen right wrist [1-5], a split lip [1], scraped right knuckles [1]. These belong in textures or decals, not in the base model.
- Later looks: short hair and a charcoal field outfit in Veyr [8, visual bible]. A scar below the left eye appears from Chapter 21. A wound to the right ribs comes in Chapter 30.

### Mariya, 24, grain keeper
- Dark hair tied back [1]. A braid in the visual bible, tied with cloth worn thin at the ends [27].
- Flour on her temple [1]. The skin under her thumb is split [1] and later heals as a pale line [13].
- Shawl: rust red. She wore it on the night of the attack. One end burned and she cut it on a slant [1]. From Chapter 13 a blue cloth patch mends the burned edge.
- Dress: cream linen [visual bible, cover notes].
- Props: pebble board, waxed tally board [1, 4].

### Iwang (the Hollow Ones)
- Tall, articulated body with hard pale outer struts [bible, 11]. Its silhouette is recognizable.
- Hands with too many joints and no nails [2]. Long gripping limbs [bible].
- An open torso. You see the wall behind it through the chest [2, 14, 28].
- A dark, wet, ridged knot at the collar where the neck narrows into the hollow body [2]. It is the only weak point [2, 11, 28].
- No eyes [2]. The visual bible gives it a pale, faceted, stone-like head.
- Sound: mineral clicking. Air passing through the torso makes a low, empty note [28]. Smell: burned salt [2].
- Behaviour: it thins like smoke away from a live gradient and turns solid again near the threshold or when it strikes [2, 28]. Its wound sheds dark grains, not blood [2].
- Ch 2 says "the shape unfolded a second set of limbs". I read that as legs after arms. The visual bible shows two arms and two legs.

### Samon, 61, neighbor and sluice worker
- Bent back [1]. Older, practical, few words [1, 2].
- Wool tunic [2]. Works with rope and baskets [1, 2].
- His left leg is crushed below the knee at the end of Chapter 2 [2]. After that he wears a light brace [4] and walks with a stick or cane [5, 27].

### Darran, 39, Telassari surveyor and overseer
- Narrow straps of a leather surveyor's cap leave marks on his cheeks [3]. He wears no bronze [3].
- A leather tube at his waist holds a hide drawing with a pierced circle [1, 2, 3].
- Carries a three-wicked oil lamp [1].
- Clean tunic even below ground [10]. A cloak with his office seal, clean at the shoulder and muddy at the hem [28].

### Lios, 11, water boy
- Narrow face, too young for harvest work [1, 2].
- A rope around his neck with freshly rubbed skin beneath it [1, 2]. The rope is cut off at the end of Chapter 2.
- Carries a clay water jug that breaks in Chapter 2 [2]. Later a cup made from its base [4, 7].

### Telassari soldier (generic spearman)
- Thick padded tunic, spear, hide shield [1]. Boots [1].
- Telassar's colour is red cloth [1]. The officer has a bronze corselet over padded cloth [1]. Captain Therren wears a red cloak [27].
- The seal is a bull with its head lowered [1].
