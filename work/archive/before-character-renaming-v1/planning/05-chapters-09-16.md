# CHAPTER CARDS 9–16

Same scene-card contract as Chapters 1–8. Every chapter has three scenes; a scene may contain a short, explicitly dated transition. Close POV and tense stay unchanged.

## 9. The Unbroken Edge
**POV:** Toby. **Day:** 18. **Target:** 2,800. **Places:** memorial gallery, calibration room, practice floor. **Function:** deliver the legendary sword while separating symbolic worth from inherited superiority. **Entry:** a novice has earned supervised access, not command.

1. **A legend with omissions.** Toby wants to understand the object he is expected to carry. Panji shows Severance, publicly called the Unbroken Edge, beneath names of earlier Wardens. A gallery account credits one hero with an evacuation; Ipin quietly identifies the maintenance crews left out of the inscription. Toby is moved by the legend despite noticing its simplification. Describe repaired grip and austere blade before its light. The sword must feel old, cared for, and consequential.
2. **Acceptance is a responsibility.** Calibration confirms that the root recognizes Toby's acquired reference. Another trainee can lift the blade but cannot establish its live connection; this is technical compatibility, not a test of virtue. Ipin explains the seven-day idle transfer procedure, naming the substitute regulator that would have to be installed. Toby asks whether someone better could do it. Panji admits yes, given safe conditions and time; training the existing reference is currently simpler. Toby elects to continue.
3. **The first real cut.** Toby attempts a guarded field cut on an inert test barrier. Three heartbeats feel long while his own protection falls. He fails once, accepts Ipin's cover, then succeeds. The thirty-second settling interval prevents an immediate second cut. Sus calls him useful; Toby hears something closer to necessary. End on pleasure in that recognition, with the reader able to see its danger.

**Exit:** Toby carries a supervised relic and is vulnerable to praise. **Setup/payoff:** the final fifteen-beat cut is mentioned as a terminal engineering function, not yet a required suicide mission; lost reference cannot be rebuilt. **Guardrail:** nobody proclaims royal ancestry, a moral selection, or invincibility. Public admiration may tempt Toby without turning him into a celebrity overnight.

## 10. The Ninth Step
**POV:** Maria. **Day:** 21. **Target:** 2,600. **Places:** levy yard, Receiver stair, coastal store. **Function:** turn suspicion into usable geography. **Entry:** her first marked message has entered a slow supply chain; she has no answer yet.

1. **Trade an ordinary skill.** Maria offers to reconcile meal allocations so she can enter the worksite. Daran permits a supervised visit because missing rations threaten productivity. She checks actual recipients against the board, memorizes a discrepancy, and must leave before copying everything. Introduce Thesa, Pell, and Anet alive through specific requests: a boot repair, a share for a child, a complaint about stale water. Hessa quietly reties a leaking water skin and passes it back, establishing a living person well before her later decline. They are people before they become losses.
2. **Learn a pattern without understanding the machine.** On the ninth stair, water in a cracked bowl trembles before a baffle opens. A guard waits through three clicks before crossing a painted seam. Maria tries to move during the wrong interval and is sharply pulled back. She revises her observation rather than instantly understanding it. Daran explains only enough to keep his laborers productive. The ancient guards are using repeatable instructions supplied by future contractors.
3. **Build a route outside authority.** At the coastal store, Maria exchanges a ration tally with a neighboring grain carrier. She learns which path avoids the citadel toll and which household can shelter an injured person. A second marked wrapper records the stair pattern and names. Her goal becomes a reliable contact and escape network, not catching a villain in a confession. Lio is assigned a safe, short delivery within the village.

**Exit:** Maria knows routes and timings; Daran now knows she counts carefully. **Emotional movement:** suspicion becomes watchful commitment. **Guardrail:** she does not yet know the ultimate purpose of the enclosure. The ninth step is a physical observation point, not a mystical number or final secret switch.

## 11. A Useful Victory
**POV:** Toby. **Day:** 28. **Target:** 2,800. **Places:** cooling station, maintenance walk, Ipin's kitchen. **Function:** let the new life genuinely reward Toby before the collapse.

1. **Work under supervision.** A local phase incursion traps a maintenance team between powered doors. Sus directs an intervention with a mapped route and known load; Toby is assigned one cut and withdrawal. He wants to prove himself but remembers the practice defeat. Ipin identifies the correct seam. Toby waits for cover, cuts it, and brings out a frightened worker rather than pursuing an Iwang. Success comes from learned procedure and teamwork.
2. **See the attractive shortcut.** Sus demonstrates a brief higher-gain setting after the evacuation, using an isolated artificial test load. It resets more quickly but heats the receiver. He does not pretend the technique has no cost. Toby sees why emergency operators would want it. Panji disputes extending it to unmapped living loads; the disagreement is specific, public enough for Toby to hear, and unresolved.
3. **An evening not devoted to destiny.** Ipin feeds Toby a meal he thinks is simple; Toby finds its texture strange and improves it with an ancient preparation. Each admits an ordinary embarrassment. Toby speaks of Maria as someone whose judgment he misses, not merely someone he must save. Ipin admits wanting work where a correct repair is enough. Their friendship acquires pleasure, making its later strain matter.

**Exit:** earned confidence, stronger friendship, a credible temptation. **Payoff:** Chapter 8's withdrawal discipline works. **Guardrail:** Sus is not obviously faking the rescue or staging a monster attack; the saved workers remain real witnesses and later matter to Eda.

## 12. What the Records Keep
**POV:** Toby. **Days:** 35–40, marked transitions. **Target:** 2,900. **Places:** dispatch desk, manifest station, calibration gallery. **Function:** convert homesickness into an evidence-driven return mission.

1. **Receive a voice from home.** Maria's bundled wrappers arrive through the scheduled physical supply run, delayed by inspections and routing. Her leaf mark authenticates them; household details supply stronger corroboration than a symbol anyone could copy. Toby wants an immediate crossing. Ipin first compares the names against service records and finds sixty biological loads beneath a protected-person count of zero. The contradiction is visible in two columns, including a familiar name.
2. **Test the first mystery.** Toby presses Panji about the opening vision and whether it could reveal how to save people. The diagnostic record shows a forty-one-second acquisition anomaly; no full film survives. Panji explains the single end-to-inception echo predicted by this damaged reference, with neither selectable date nor controllable replay. Toby realizes the vision may concern his own future closure, but not when or whether he survives. Do not conceal this deduction from the close POV.
3. **Obtain a limited mission.** Toby, Ipin, and Panji present the count discrepancy. Sus authorizes a Day-42 inspection and repair with a narrow safety mandate, retaining remote field command. He needs the reference healthy and the branch productive; an investigation may also protect his operation from uncontrolled failure. Toby accepts the restrictions to get home, privately intending to intervene if the counts are people. That intention makes the later violation a choice.

**Exit:** a return is scheduled with explicit limits and a meaningful reason Toby might break them. **Guardrail:** do not treat the entire Concord as knowing a single perfect secret. Ipin has accessed his own manifest layer, not every command file. The message delay predates a working personal relay.

## 13. The First Crossing Back
**POV:** Toby. **Day:** 42. **Target:** 3,300. **Places:** Salt Gate, Aras yard, Receiver annex. **Function:** midpoint reversal; attempted rescue produces captivity. **Entry:** authorized inspection, not a rescue army.

1. **Return to changed people.** Toby expects relief; Maria asks what he has actually learned and what he can promise. They share an unhurried moment of physical affection before urgent work, acknowledging an attachment already present. Saman's brace, empty households, and changed routines make six weeks visible. Ipin gives Maria a survey wafer that can buffer messages for the service relay; he shows its power limit and diagnostic schedule. This is equipment for inspection, not a miracle gift chosen for a future crisis.
2. **Recognize the human load.** In the annex, the party finds sixty people inside the marked load area, including Maria, who has come with a food delivery. Daran calls them temporary labor. Toby sees the restraint geometry tighten when someone tries to leave. Panji calls for mapping and disengagement before release. Toby instead opens the visibly occupied branch when a guard threatens a worker; twelve escape into the safe stair. His immediate compassion is real, and his decision ignores a specific redistribution warning.
3. **Pay for incomplete knowledge.** The load shifts into the adjoining zone, tethering the other forty-eight, including Maria, Lio, Neris, and Oran. Iwang crowd the active seams. Panji prevents Toby from repeating a cut that would crush the remaining enclosure; Sus orders withdrawal. Toby retreats with the twelve rescued people and his team through Salt Gate after local villagers take the freed group. Maria retains the wafer inside. Both see the other remain alive; there is no manufactured belief that she died.

**Exit:** twelve saved; forty-eight trapped; Toby's homeward triumph is destroyed. **Plant/payoff:** water-route redirection, bounded sword power, physical wafer, moral flaw of unilateral rescue. **Guardrail:** sixty is the total including Maria, not sixty plus her. None of the forty-eight are transported to the future. Make the redistribution legible in space before describing horror.

## 14. Inside the Hollow
**POV:** Maria. **Days:** 42–44. **Target:** 2,900. **Places:** annex shelter, water channel, inner baffle. **Function:** establish captivity as a social and logistical conflict with an active viewpoint.

1. **Make a living count.** Maria counts forty-eight, records injuries, and organizes water collection. Daran's guards deliver rations because live anchors are required; the Iwang are routed by field baffles, not obedient servants. Neris rejects Maria's attempt to do every job. Oran wants the strongest to try an immediate escape; Pell and Anet argue from different household obligations. Maria proposes a small test before moving the group.
2. **A test with a loss.** Using the stair pattern, they move a rope through a promising route during a quiet interval. An unstable ledge fails before the return interval; Thesa falls into the active boundary and dies despite an attempted recovery. Do not make her death a punishment for disobedience. The test reveals both a usable service recess and the impossibility of moving tethered people without branch release. Maria stops a second attempt and gives the reason to everyone.
3. **Send facts instead of reassurance.** Maria buffers a message: forty-seven alive, one dead, water and ration status, the recess, and the timing pattern. A narrow diagnostic pulse confirms only that the wafer handed the packet to the relay, not that Toby has read it. She tells the group what is known and still overstates how soon help might come. End on Neris asking what happens if the promised interval passes.

**Exit:** forty-seven living captives; a tested limitation and one useful route. **Emotional movement:** shock → purposeful control → guilt at premature certainty. **Guardrail:** Iwang do not imitate Thesa's voice or become her soul. Grief gets human silence, not an invented monster ability.

## 15. The Price of Opening
**POV:** Toby. **Day:** 44. **Target:** 2,700. **Places:** debrief room, clinic, private offer chamber. **Function:** make responsibility specific without validating Sus's entire policy.

1. **An account he cannot evade.** Toby demands rescue. Sus reconstructs the failed cut with branch telemetry and twelve successful evacuations beside forty-eight stranded. Toby initially blames the system, then admits he ignored a warning. Panji refuses to call the mistake malice but does not absolve it. Ipin retrieves Maria's packet: forty-seven remain. A death has a name. Toby cannot honestly repeat 'at least we saved some' as a complete answer.
2. **Look at the cost.** Eda examines field injuries and disputes the protected-person category. She can provide medical observations, not command a full intervention yet. Refugee testimony from the twelve freed people is held by Maria's local network and can be sought through the supply route; do not magically produce them in her clinic. The future workers saved in Chapter 11 offer directly available corroboration of field effects.
3. **A persuasive smaller mercy.** Sus offers a carefully limited extraction prioritizing Maria if Toby supports the anchor program. He argues that known capacity is better than an unplanned rescue, and cites Toby's actual failure. Toby does not agree to abandon everyone, but asks how the improved setting works. This keeps temptation active instead of turning him into an instantly perfect dissident. End with Ipin asking whose names are absent from Sus's count.

**Exit:** Toby is culpable, grieving, and tempted by selective rescue; an alternative needs evidence and capacity. **Guardrail:** Sus's offer must have a real technical route and political exception, not be an obvious lie. Maria is alive and her wishes have not been consulted.

## 16. The Line Beneath the Seal
**POV:** Toby. **Day:** 46. **Target:** 2,700. **Places:** maintenance archive, memorial passage, Panji's workroom threshold. **Function:** expose institutional continuity and break uncomplicated mentor trust.

1. **A bounded search.** Ipin can inspect old maintenance authorizations because the current fault shares an index. He cannot open protected command records. Toby helps match the evacuation memorial's date to the service log; neither solves it by hacking everything. A sealed route from twelve years earlier carried ninety-seven unregistered people while the official rescue report counted none.
2. **Find a familiar signature.** Panji authorized the sealing. Sus's command record confirms implementation, and Ipin recognizes a later form he himself has signed. The discovery links present practice to ordinary administrative obedience. Toby wants to publish immediately; Ipin points out what the evidence proves and what it does not. They retain an authenticated partial copy and identify the missing witness rather than inventing certainty.
3. **Choose confrontation.** Toby goes to Panji carrying the discrepancy, not a drawn sword. Panji sees the old date and does not deny recognizing it. End on the direct question of whether the ninety-seven were alive when he closed the route. The next chapter must answer promptly; do not stretch the cliffhanger into a sequence of evasions.

**Exit:** trust is suspended; Panji must account for a past choice. **Setup/payoff:** omitted gallery workers, false person-counts, Ipin's permissions, Panji's protective paternalism. **Guardrail:** the ninety-seven are a separate past incident, never added to the current forty-seven. No recovered diary conveniently explains Sus's entire personality.
