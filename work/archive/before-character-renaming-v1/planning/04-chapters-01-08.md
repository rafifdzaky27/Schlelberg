# CHAPTER CARDS 1–8

Each numbered scene specifies goal/tactic, opposition, turn/decision, consequence, and emotional purpose. Setup/payoff references correspond to the reveal ledger. Treat titles as working titles. Targets guide proportion, not padding. Do not add scenes that only repeat the current emotion.

## 1. The King's Measure
**POV:** Toby. **Day:** 0, ninth day of occupation. **Target:** existing ~2,817 words; revise to 2,800–3,100. **Places:** upper granary, its rear yard, underground Receiver. **Function:** establish useful ordinary competence, political defeat, and the temporal hook. **Entry state:** occupied but still rooted in home.

1. **Seed levy.** Toby and Maria want three of fourteen seed jars retained. She negotiates with the levy officer; Toby identifies an enlarged measure and then physically resists the seizure of the leaf-marked jar. Seven soldiers make strength without training useless. He is injured and reassigned to forced drainage labor. Two jars remain. His resistance is understandable but fails; Maria's work matters.
2. **Care and disagreement.** Behind the granary, Maria cleans his wounds. He objects to her having supplied his name; she explains the labor demand and refuses his fantasy that a sword alone would have solved the encounter. He notices her hunger and offers crushed bread. Affection survives disagreement without declaring a romance resolved.
3. **Receiver.** Toby clears a drain; his knowledge makes the overseer wait. Workers depart by order. Conductive contact acquires Toby; an image of a future city and his injured older-seeming self appears. Preserve no-crossing evidence: still water, no actual wind. Apply the exact warning patch from the audit. End before a physical crossing.

**Exit state:** wounded forced laborer confronting a possible future self; no supernatural combat power. **Plant:** grain/record discrepancy, shield defeat, Maria's practical agency, old stone geometry, overseer's diagram, left-eye scar/right-rib injury in echo. **Guardrail:** no narrator knowledge of the Concord or Schlelberg.

## 2. The Door Without Wind
**POV:** Toby. **Day:** 0, continuous. **Target:** 2,700. **Function:** distinguish echo from gate and make the first extraordinary consequence costly. **Entry:** overseer gripping Toby, guard at stairs, Maria absent.

1. **Test the image.** Toby wants to reach the wounded man; the overseer orders him restrained. A dropped tool strikes the black plane despite the visible street. The last warning hangs in the chamber; no additional warning is spoken, and the image dies at the forty-one-second limit. Toby sees physical evidence that this state cannot be entered. The preceding crash was the water boy's broken jug in the passage, not an unexplained second attack.
2. **A live opening.** The overseer follows his drawing and forces the damaged clamp into position. Real air now moves; an Iwang crosses from the paired current future, not from the echo's later scene. Its open torso and collar nexus are visible only through action. A guard's spear injures it when it solidifies to strike; his shield does not make him safe. Toby saves the water boy, Lio, instead of escaping alone. He cannot kill the creature by willpower.
3. **Close the route.** Saman returns with workers after hearing the struggle. Toby uses a rope and worksite lever to dislodge the clamp while others clear the stair. The creature withdraws through the narrowing opening; falling masonry crushes Saman's left leg. One guard is killed. Daran secures the site and orders Toby detained, recognizing his operational value.

**Exit:** gate temporarily closed; Toby confined, Saman injured, Lio alive outside with villagers. **Change:** Toby's knowledge saved lives, but extraordinary access has endangered home. **Guardrail:** do not introduce a sword, future shield for Toby, or unexplained spontaneous fire. Inception speech is over; do not keep asking the future self questions.

## 3. A Blade of Quiet Light
**POV:** Toby. **Day:** 1. **Target:** 2,900. **Places:** detention store, worksite, courtyard. **Function:** earn recruitment through a testable bargain rather than prophecy.

1. **Bargain under detention.** Toby wants Saman treated and demands answers from Daran. Daran wants him to repeat activation; his imperfect instructions reveal that he is not a master of the phenomenon. The levy captain Theren prevents wasteful punishment while awaiting the commissioned specialist. Distinguish the two men's authority and motives.
2. **Panji arrives.** Alarm telemetry from the functioning auxiliary brings Panji; his credentials belong to the technical-services arrangement already used by Daran. He tests the sealed site, identifies the Iwang by name, and blocks an angry guard's strike with a visible field. When he uses a blade to cut a fouled component, his protection drops. The moment shows a rule and danger, not a stylish slaughter. Toby notices the same institutional mark on Panji's authorization and Daran's drawing.
3. **A conditional offer.** Panji needs a living reference trained before another opening. Toby refuses to leave for an abstract world-saving mission. He demands care for Saman, release of Lio, and replacement grain delivered before his departure. Panji accepts only what he can provide, admits that general evacuation is not authorized, and arranges it. Toby agrees to discuss the offer with Maria, not to vanish.

**Exit:** conditional cooperation, not trust. **New knowledge:** other world is reachable; Iwang are physical; Toby's contact changed the receiver. **Plant:** Panji's actual service, limited authority, shield vulnerability, shared insignia. **Guardrail:** Panji cannot name the ending, know every future event, or heal by a touch.

## 4. Two Jars
**POV:** Maria. **Day:** 1 evening–2 morning. **Target:** 2,700. **Function:** give home an independent plot and test the recruitment promises.

1. **Account for damage.** Maria wants enough food and hands to survive the next week. She hears inconsistent reports of the underground event; counts the dead guard separately from village casualties and observes Saman's injury. Neris, the midwife, insists Maria eat before assigning more tasks. Introduce a life outside Toby through disagreement over store shares and a household omitted from the levy list.
2. **Verify the stranger.** Panji's supplies arrive: actual grain and dressings, not a promissory speech. Maria checks quantities and tests the treatment on an ordinary task with Saman's assent. She challenges Panji about transport for other villagers. His refusal is administrative; let her name that distinction. She sees that accepting help need not mean believing everything he says.
3. **Make a separate commitment.** Maria and Toby discuss departure. She wants him to learn and return with a useful plan, not claim everyone will be safe. They acknowledge wanting a life together; affection has texture—shared work, irritation, familiarity. She elects to remain and monitor people assigned to the Receiver. They agree on a leaf mark for authenticated supply messages and a next contact window.

**Exit:** Maria has a monitoring and survival objective. **Plant:** Neris, leaf-mark authentication, two jars and wider food pressure, labor tallies. **Guardrail:** no omniscient disclosure of Panji's secret. No lover demands unconditional loyalty or waits without acting.

## 5. The Far Shore
**POV:** Toby. **Day:** 2. **Target:** 3,000. **Places:** Aras path, Salt Gate, Veyr arrival. **Function:** irreversible departure and earned wonder.

1. **Leave something unfinished.** Toby helps secure the damaged site before leaving; the injured wrist limits him. Saman accepts medicine but asks who will repair the sluice. Maria already has a work arrangement, demonstrating that home will not cease to function without Toby. The farewell acknowledges fear and attraction without promising a guaranteed date of victory.
2. **Cross through the auxiliary.** Panji takes him to the coast by an established walking route. Toby asks why they do not use the village door; Panji answers damage and safety. At Salt Gate, show mass crossing through an unmistakably physical state, distinct from the echo. Toby consents after seeing the return register and learning paired clocks advance together. Do not invoke destiny to bypass his choice.
3. **Arrive as a dependent person.** Veyr's vertical streets, regulated air, and unfamiliar food production overwhelm him. He cannot read signs. His guide translates rather than mocking him. A transport interruption reveals maintenance and waiting people beneath the spectacle. Panji takes him to a clinic rather than directly to a magic armory.

**Exit:** Toby has crossed; practical needs remain. **Question created:** what will he owe for this help? **Plant:** return procedures, material future life, inability to read future script. **Guardrail:** no time jump that erases the two days passed at home.

## 6. A City That Does Not Sleep
**POV:** Toby. **Days:** 3–5. **Target:** 3,100. **Function:** establish future society, Ipin, Eda, and Sus's credibility.

1. **Care with boundaries.** Eda examines wrist, ribs, and lip, explains treatment and recovery, and treats another patient without regard to registration. Toby meets Ipin, sent to inventory his field exposure; he objects to being handled as equipment. Their conflict is mundane and specific. Ipin shows how translation works and what it cannot do.
2. **A service failure.** During a permitted transit tour, a carrier platform stalls above a maintenance tier. Sus organizes a controlled rescue, assigns cover while lowering his shield, and admits the limit of a damaged device. Toby sees him risk himself for workers. Ipin contributes a bounded technical fact. The rescue must involve a discernible sequence, not 'he moved impossibly fast.'
3. **The actual offer.** Panji and Sus explain the damaged root, the need to train a temporary reference, and the prospect of reopening a safe return route. They show a map with three receiver sites. Initial deadline forecasts are provisional. Toby accepts training but asks about the people near the sites; he gets an incomplete policy answer, not a clear lie everyone should instantly recognize. Explain the observed Chapter-2 Iwang retreat: away from a live gradient, a creature cannot remain coherent for long. Seed Eda's separate civil casualty-response role through a routine authorization she handles at the clinic.

**Exit:** Toby wants the competence Sus has demonstrated; Ipin is reluctant assigned support. **Plant:** Eda's independence, service routes, shield-cover rhythm, civic vulnerability, three-site map. **Guardrail:** neither mentor knows the complete echo. Do not label Sus sinister.

## 7. Names on Clay
**POV:** Maria. **Days:** 6–7. **Target:** 2,500. **Function:** occupation progresses while Toby learns; establish the missing-person evidence.

1. **Meet current hunger.** Maria allocates household food and considers the protected seed. A child and an elderly household have no remaining share. She spends a measured portion from one reserved jar without first obtaining all households' consent, then records it privately. Neris challenges her habit of making difficult decisions alone.
2. **Trace laborers.** At the mill and well, Maria collects names of people sent below. Official totals disagree with families' accounts. Daran supplies enough mundane paperwork to appear legitimate. Lio identifies a guard's route because he carried water there; he gives an observation, not a child's brilliant solution to the plot.
3. **Send a test.** Maria marks a supply wrapper with the agreed leaf and exact discrepancies, arranging its passage through Panji's scheduled delivery. She does not yet accuse every outsider of collusion. She contacts a neighboring household network about alternate grain and coastal access. That practical network will matter during the political aftermath.

**Exit:** a verifiable message exists; Maria has concealed a seed decision while investigating concealed labor. **Emotional move:** strain into purposeful suspicion. **Guardrail:** her arithmetic competence is specific; she cannot decipher Maker diagrams or future records.

## 8. The Weight of a Shield
**POV:** Toby. **Days:** 6–16, an explicitly marked training interval. **Target:** 3,000. **Function:** visible, limited skill growth and developing friendship.

1. **Fail for a reason.** Sus teaches stance, distance, and the shield-down interval. Toby's farm strength makes him overcommit; his injured wrist prevents sustained blade work. He loses to a smaller trainee because he exposes the same opening. The correction is technical, not 'believe in yourself.' Eda's restrictions remain binding.
2. **A different kind of work.** Panji teaches observation and retreat in a repair exercise. Toby's understanding of water routes helps predict a redirected flow, but he mistakes a powered valve for a manual one and has to ask Ipin. They repair it together. Rest, food, and humor provide real relief; include something each enjoys that is unrelated to saving the world.
3. **A modest win.** Toby completes a timed cover-and-withdrawal exercise with Ipin operating a shield stand. He resists the urge to chase a disarmed opponent and protects the assigned exit. Panji praises the specific decision; Sus points out the speed still missing. Passing grants access to the relic demonstration, not mastery.

**Exit:** physically improving, still novice; Ipin begins treating him as a coworker. **Plant/payoff:** Chapter 1's shield defeat becomes a trainable problem; partner cover rehearses the finale. Include an ordinary haircut during recovery so the later echo's shorter hair is earned. Introduce Jora and Senn as trained operators assisting practice, without creating two new subplots. **Guardrail:** no montage claiming months in ten days; right-wrist strain has improved, not vanished.
