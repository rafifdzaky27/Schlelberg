### What happens in the intervals

Days 17, 19–20, 22–27, 29–34, and 41 are ordinary recovery, supervised practice, duty, and scheduled transit preparation, not unwritten major adventures. Mention only details that establish change. Days 45 and 50–55 include care and evidence work. Days 79–81 position equipment, confirm safe shelter, wait for the forecast lower-load interval, and obtain civic access enforcement. Do not invent a second quest in these gaps.

Earliest *safe* mass release is Day 82: after Day 57 the branch is too unstable for a mass untethering until a measured low-load trough that lasts into Day 83. Individual emergency extractions remain technically possible at high redistributed cost. Forecast this trough in Chapter 24, corroborate it with Maria's baffle timings, and remind the reader in Chapter 25. This explains why the coalition prepares instead of waiting inexplicably, why Sus can still offer a selective exception, and why seven idle days cannot fit after evacuation. The Day-84 convergence is the next irreversible rise, not an arbitrary deadline chosen by the rescuers.

## Permissions and responsibility

Sus commands Wardens and holds root operations authority. Panji has training/calibration authority, with field credentials for ancient access. Ipin has maintenance/manifest permissions, never universal admin access. Eda is a physician who also serves as a registered civil casualty-response convener: independent review, temporary service protection, and referral to civic marshals fall within that role. She cannot convict Sus, rule the Concord, or unilaterally operate the root. The civic review assembles corroboration and an enforceable access order during Chapters 24–25; a split chain of command prevents immediate effortless arrest. Marshals are introduced before the final fight.

Theren controls native levies and garrison decisions within his commission. Daran controls the worksite under a technical concession; he does not command the whole kingdom. Maria can negotiate because people choose to work with her and her counts are credible, not because an ancient society suddenly elects a modern president.
