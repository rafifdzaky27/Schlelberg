# CHAPTER CARDS 17–24

## 17. The Terms of Mercy
**POV:** Tobious. **Day:** 47. **Target:** 2,900. **Places:** Panjios's workroom, calibration station, operations room. **Function:** answer the betrayal, establish the firm deadline, and make the shortcut a conscious moral temptation. **Entry:** Tobious holds a partial record of ninety-seven abandoned people.

1. **An answer, not an excuse.** Panjios confirms that the ninety-seven were alive. A failing route threatened a populated future ward; he accepted Suss's count and sealed it, then helped preserve the official description. He believed necessity justified silence. Tobious asks why he was not told before accepting the sword. Panjios admits fear of losing his cooperation. Tobious rejects renewed unquestioning trust but keeps the facts and does not pretend Panjios's past kindness was unreal.
2. **A deadline that can be checked.** Ypin compares field stress against Edda's independent exposure observations. Both support Day 84 as the final viable disconnect window. The data already show rising load with shorter troughs between peaks; later planning will use one of those troughs, not invent a new calendar rule. An idle seven-day reference transfer would require releasing the captives first; shutting the system down around them would kill them. Tobious asks why the root cannot simply be destroyed now. Panjios explains tether death and future infrastructure dependence. The rescue and civil preparation must precede the final cut.
3. **Consent with a dangerous gap.** Suss proposes a controlled high-gain trial followed by a limited rescue. He admits operator injury and temporary network load, while suppressing how that load is assigned to captive bodies. Tobious consents to risk himself and demands measurements. His mistake is accepting an incomplete definition of 'network'; it is not agreeing to torture people with full knowledge. He requires contact with Mariya before a live extraction. Suss grants one supervised diagnostic window.

**Exit:** certainty about time, broken mentor trust, conditional participation. **Emotional movement:** betrayal → anger → desire to regain control through useful action. **Guardrail:** no new apocalyptic clock in later chapters; forecast precision increases but the deadline stays Day 84. Panjios's confession occupies a scene, not an entire retrospective novella.

## 18. A Table with Three Places
**POV:** Tobious. **Day:** 48. **Target:** 2,600. **Places:** relay booth, adjoining service room, Ypin's kitchen. **Function:** deepen love and establish Mariya's informed refusal of private salvation before the final separation.

1. **Hear the actual person.** The diagnostic window opens as scheduled. Tobious receives Mariya's voice with gaps but enough clarity for conversation. She reports forty-seven living and the name of the dead. He offers the limited extraction. She asks who would take the additional load if she left; he cannot answer. She refuses to consent to abandoning the others for her. They disagree directly; she is neither serenely selfless nor angry that he wants her alive.
2. **A life they would like.** In the remaining contact time, they talk about what they miss: finishing a meal without counting portions, the sound of the sluice after repair, sleeping without listening for steps. They acknowledge love in their own plain language. Tobious promises to work for the whole group and admits he cannot promise success. Mariya says what help is useful—water, release order, an honest timetable. The connection ends on the known power limit, not conveniently before a necessary confession.
3. **Room for another person.** Over a meal, Ypin asks why Tobious keeps accepting all the danger himself. Tobious gives an imperfect answer. The spare place is practical, left from a colleague's visit, and becomes associated with the life Tobious imagines Mariya might share; do not turn it into an authorial symbol everyone discusses. Ypin admits signing biological-load forms without asking what they carried. Friendship now includes mutual accountability.

**Exit:** love is explicit; Mariya's independent allegiance is known; Tobious's rescue goal is the whole group. **Guardrail:** romance is not a reward for sword skill. No vow to die together, possessive threat, or assurance that love will defeat time.

## 19. The Unbarred Edge
**POV:** Tobious. **Days:** 49–55. **Target:** 2,800. **Places:** guarded practice chamber, cooling branch, equipment bench. **Function:** show why the forbidden gain works well enough to seduce him.

1. **Train the cost honestly.** Tobious practices with measured increments and rest under Edda's restrictions. His progress is control under pressure, not mastery of Suss's whole discipline. The gain bypass shortens the thirty-second settling interval but leaves tremor and thermal load. Panjios argues for abandoning it. Tobious distrusts the mentor who withheld ninety-seven lives and accepts only independently measured objections; a justified loss of trust now makes good advice harder to use.
2. **A success that narrows judgment.** A controlled field trial becomes dangerous when a damaged cooling shutter traps Ypin and a crew member. Tobious uses the bypass to complete two cuts in succession and gets both out. The local test load was supposed to remain artificial; command routing has quietly connected it to the broader root. Tobious sees a brief unexplained receiver spike but no visible captive injury. Suss praises the result and attributes the spike to settling. The rescue is real, not a staged threat.
3. **Choose not to ask enough.** Ypin requests the full routing trace. Tobious delays filing the anomaly because he fears suspension will lose their rescue window, privately telling himself one more measured operation will clarify it. He agrees to Day-57 live work while insisting on the group rescue objective. The moral failure is legible: useful results tempt him to treat uncertain harm elsewhere as secondary. End with the hidden trace still on his private device.

**Exit:** more capable, more compromised, physically strained. **Payoff:** Suss's Chapter-11 demonstration and Panjios's damaged credibility. **Guardrail:** no possession, whispering sword, intoxication that removes responsibility, or sudden new ability unrelated to cutting a field.

## 20. A Name for the Missing
**POV:** Mariya. **Day:** 56. **Target:** 2,800. **Places:** captive shelter, ration hatch, service recess. **Function:** reveal the human price before Tobious understands its full cause.

1. **A surge in bodies.** A pulse interrupts ordinary water distribution. Captives convulse or lose coordination; the injury is frightening through selected detail, not prolonged spectacle. Pehl, already weakened, dies during the surge. Annet survives it but dies later from accumulated strain and inadequate care. Give each a distinct final human interaction tied to the person introduced in Chapter 10. Mariya does not acquire medical powers. Forty-five remain.
2. **Account for her own decisions.** Rations tighten while Darran insists the system is being stabilized. Orran proposes moving the stronger people first and concealing the plan from those who cannot keep up. Nerissa asks Mariya to stop promising a date she cannot guarantee. Mariya admits the uncertain timetable and that she spent protected seed without shared consent. Some defend the choice; others object to the secrecy. She accepts a small distribution council rather than demanding gratitude for keeping them alive.
3. **Send a warning she cannot force through.** Mariya records the two deaths, the surge timing, and signs of linked operations. The receiver's overloaded diagnostic lane cannot complete its scheduled transfer. She sees the failure indication and stores the packet for the next viable poll. This is the same fault Tobious has chosen not to escalate, with command capacity diverted to the live operation. End on her instructing Nerissa and Orran how to run the count if she is incapacitated.

**Exit:** forty-five alive; Mariya has relinquished unilateral authority. **Emotional movement:** grief → exposed inadequacy → shared responsibility. **Guardrail:** seed use did not cause Pehl's or Annet's field injuries. Do not manufacture an argument that all rationing decisions are equivalent to Suss's deliberate exploitation.

## 21. What Power Takes
**POV:** Tobious. **Day:** 57. **Target:** 3,500. **Places:** active branch control, isolation door, service descent. **Function:** major failure, irreversible mentor loss, exposure of the shortcut's true cost.

1. **Follow the actual current.** Tobious begins the authorized operation expecting a mapped release. Ypin gains a live routing view and identifies the biological channel beneath the artificial-load label. Tobious recognizes names from Mariya's message beside the draw. Suss orders him to hold the gain: stopping now will damage future services. Tobious admits his unreported spike and refuses the next cut. Suss acts to restrain him and seize the blade; neither side pauses for a complete philosophical speech.
2. **A choice that cannot clean the past.** During the struggle, Tobious's lowered shield exposes him to shattered lens material, cutting beneath his left eye. Panjios uses the manual isolation door to give Ypin, Tobious, and trapped future workers a retreat. Tobious first tries a physically credible extraction of Panjios; the door has to remain held until the last workers clear. Panjios refuses another abandoned group. Suss, trying to regain live control, kills him directly at the obstruction. His death changes the escape geometry; it does not erase his culpability for the ninety-seven.
3. **Save a person instead of the perfect case.** Ypin has to leave the main archive device to free Tobious from a collapsing coupling rail. His left forearm burns. Partial signed records already kept elsewhere survive; the complete operational cache does not. They escape through the service descent introduced in Chapters 6 and 11, unable to make the alternate Quarry receiver safe. Suss broadcasts a credible partial account of Tobious's unauthorized interference. End with Severance intact, Panjios dead, Tobious and Ypin fugitives, and captives still trapped.

**Exit:** apparent defeat, grief, damaged bodies, incomplete evidence. **Guardrail:** no resurrection, lucky hidden escape tunnel, or panicked accusation unsupported by earlier failures. The right-rib wound of the opening echo has NOT happened yet; that remains Chapter 30.

## 22. No More Names
**POV:** Tobious. **Days:** 58–62. **Target:** 2,700. **Places:** lower service shelter, manual reservoir, Edda's clinic side entrance. **Function:** a sustained near-surrender arc expressed through behavior and work.

1. **Try to stop being necessary.** Tobious lays the sword out to surrender it, believing another decision from him may kill more people. He considers accepting Suss's authority in exchange for selective evacuation, rather than a melodramatic wish to disappear. Ypin refuses to deliver the blade and refuses to call Tobious blameless. They argue over his concealed spike; friendship is damaged but not reduced to a misunderstanding. Medical care and several nights of poor sleep give grief duration.
2. **Do something bounded.** A manual reservoir needs a repair while searches block normal crews. Ypin asks for Tobious's old practical skill, with a clear safety limit. Tobious cannot fix everything and needs a worker to finish the tool movement his hand cannot sustain. Water reaches several households. No applause follows. This is the first evidence that useful responsibility can be smaller than total control; it does not cure bereavement in one scene.
3. **Accept a condition instead of an absolution.** Edda will treat them and preserve testimony, but refuses a citywide intervention based only on their accusation. She needs living counts, a credible release sequence, and future backup capacity. Tobious agrees to help build those instead of demanding faith in his intentions. He does not yet retrieve a miraculous new message. End on the modest decision to attend tomorrow's planning session.

**Exit:** still grieving, no longer choosing withdrawal as his only response. **Guardrail:** Ypin does not deliver a generic motivational monologue; Mariya's love does not magically solve his culpability. Tobious must eventually disclose his own failure publicly as well as Suss's.

## 23. The Forty-Fifth Name
**POV:** Mariya. **Day:** 63. **Target:** 2,700. **Places:** annex, service recess, improvised rest area. **Function:** supply the rescue's indispensable human and spatial data.

1. **Let someone correct the list.** Nerissa reads the roll while Mariya listens. Forty-five answer in different ways; not all can stand. Hessah, a living household member briefly seen earlier among the workers, now has severe weakness and must be assessed with her consent. Give her a preference about who carries her and an ordinary belonging she wants preserved. The plan cannot pretend all forty-five can run.
2. **Choose a share of the work.** Orran volunteers for a dangerous bracing task after hearing its actual risks, changing his earlier demand to leave weaker people. Mariya does not shame him into martyrdom. They test a stretcher turn with empty weight, find the initial route too narrow, and select the wider service recess despite its longer exposure. Darran's ration schedule gives one predictable gap. Lios's task is a simple signal from cover with an adult backup.
3. **Make contact useful.** The next powered diagnostic poll succeeds. Mariya transmits forty-five names, the three deaths, Hessah's condition, baffle intervals, stretcher width, and the last stored surge warning. She receives acknowledgment of delivery, not a detailed rescue promise. She tells Nerissa she is afraid and accepts help without issuing another assignment. End with the group ready to decide on a real proposal when one arrives.

**Exit:** the captives have contributed essential planning information and shared leadership. **Payoff:** lists, baffle observation, wafer limits, Mariya's changed relation to certainty. **Guardrail:** Hessah must be seeded as an ordinary living person before this chapter; add a brief identifying action in Chapter 10 or 14. Forty-five includes Mariya.

## 24. What Remains of an Oath
**POV:** Tobious. **Days:** 64–68. **Target:** 3,000. **Places:** Edda's clinic meeting room, service model bench, root maintenance overlook. **Function:** form a viable coalition and state the permanent cost before the finale.

1. **Make the case answerable.** Mariya's packet, Ypin's retained authorizations, Edda's medical evidence, and testimony from rescued future workers establish deliberate human loading. Tobious submits his own concealed anomaly. Edda finds grounds to invoke a civil-emergency review and protect essential service crews, not instant power to convict Suss. She requests independent civic marshals and corroborating operations staff; some refuse, some agree. Support has names, limits, and obligations.
2. **Find the difficult option.** Using the original three-node map and known terminal function, the group constructs evacuation followed by permanent root destruction. Mariya's measurements make a stretcher route possible; Tobious's drainage knowledge predicts redirection; Ypin calculates intervals. The post-breach oscillation yields its earliest safe mass-release trough on Days 82–83, leaving only hours before terminal convergence. Earlier selective extractions would transfer load to those left behind. They reject a tempting shortcut when it would reroute the danger through occupied future homes. Civil preparation becomes part of the mission. This is a synthesis of established knowledge, not a newly discovered magic mode.
3. **Say the price aloud.** The final cut must be made at the future root by the current living reference. The earliest feasible evacuation leaves hours, not seven idle days to transfer it. Tobious can remain in the future afterward, alive but unable to return; Mariya could cross beforehand if she wants. He instructs the team to tell her the whole plan. He accepts permanent loss without making her choice for her. End on assigning responsibilities with the right to challenge him.

**Exit:** credible rescue coalition, fixed deadline, disclosed separation risk, consent becoming practice. **Guardrail:** the permanent cost is not hidden until Chapter 29. Edda's authority creates lawful resistance and logistical capacity, not universal obedience or a magical arrest command.
