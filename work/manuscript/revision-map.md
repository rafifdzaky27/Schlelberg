## Primary craft diagnosis

1. **The middle and climax now have fuller scenes but uneven duration.** Chapters 18, 20–23, and 26 still resolve several choices quickly. Add resistance, failed tactics, interruptions, and aftermath only where they change the reader's understanding; remove repeated explanations of the same ethical principle.
2. **Institutional material can still feel like meetings.** Records matter only when attached to a known person, a threatened service, a practical refusal, or a change in authority. Let Ypin, Edda, and ordinary workers physically handle consequences and disagree from their own stakes.
3. **Mariya's captivity has stronger relationships, but some deaths still arrive after brief appearances.** Keep the cooperation, annoyance, and care around Thesia, Pehl, Annet, Orran, and Hessah concrete. Do not add arbitrary deaths.
4. **Tobious's expertise must remain bounded.** He has reference compatibility and learned procedure, not elite sword mastery. Expand the texture of preparation, fear during shield-down windows, corrections by others, and his habit of claiming decisions when frightened.
5. **The future needs more ordinary desire.** Veyr is beautiful and functional, but later scenes emphasize records and crisis. Add daily work, care, food, music, weather, unequal access, and people who would want the city to survive for reasons beyond Suss's calculation.
6. **The separation must breathe.** Chapters 26 and 29 establish informed choice correctly but too quickly. Deepen Mariya's credible attraction to Veyr and her desire to live in Aras apart from being indispensable; let Tobious want a shared life without trying to own her choice. Keep the permanent ending.
7. **The 45-person evacuation now has explicit continuity.** Keep the overnight staging and dawn departure legible in the final edit; the two deaths occur amid attempts to save Orran and Hessah. Darran remains separate. No Iwang power, sword mode, portal, or shield shortcut should appear just to solve a beat.
8. **Quiet aftermath is part of the climax.** Deepen the split-command detention, the fifteen-heartbeat root cut, city losses including three skyway deaths, Tobious's physical adaptation, the limited Telassari charter, and Mariya's ordinary future.
