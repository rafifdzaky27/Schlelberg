The future has a visible civilian and maintenance life; Suss's help is genuine before his selective accounting is exposed. Severance is technically compatible rather than morally choosing Tobious. The main draft weakness is compression: several chapters now read closer to substantial scenes than to full novel chapters, so final scope and emotional duration are not yet met. Preserve the event order while deepening grief, work, training, pleasure, and consequences.
