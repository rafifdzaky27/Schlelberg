# CHAPTER CARDS 9–16

Same scene-card contract as Chapters 1–8. Every chapter has three scenes; a scene may contain a short, explicitly dated transition. Close POV and tense stay unchanged.

## 9. The Unbroken Edge
**POV:** Tobious. **Day:** 18. **Target:** 2,800. **Places:** memorial gallery, calibration room, practice floor. **Function:** deliver the legendary sword while separating symbolic worth from inherited superiority. **Entry:** a novice has earned supervised access, not command.

1. **A legend with omissions.** Tobious wants to understand the object he is expected to carry. Panjios shows Severance, publicly called the Unbroken Edge, beneath names of earlier Wardens. A gallery account credits one hero with an evacuation; Ypin quietly identifies the maintenance crews left out of the inscription. Tobious is moved by the legend despite noticing its simplification. Describe repaired grip and austere blade before its light. The sword must feel old, cared for, and consequential.
2. **Acceptance is a responsibility.** Calibration confirms that the root recognizes Tobious's acquired reference. Another trainee can lift the blade but cannot establish its live connection; this is technical compatibility, not a test of virtue. Ypin explains the seven-day idle transfer procedure, naming the substitute regulator that would have to be installed. Tobious asks whether someone better could do it. Panjios admits yes, given safe conditions and time; training the existing reference is currently simpler. Tobious elects to continue.
3. **The first real cut.** Tobious attempts a guarded field cut on an inert test barrier. Three heartbeats feel long while his own protection falls. He fails once, accepts Ypin's cover, then succeeds. The thirty-second settling interval prevents an immediate second cut. Suss calls him useful; Tobious hears something closer to necessary. End on pleasure in that recognition, with the reader able to see its danger.

**Exit:** Tobious carries a supervised relic and is vulnerable to praise. **Setup/payoff:** the final fifteen-beat cut is mentioned as a terminal engineering function, not yet a required suicide mission; lost reference cannot be rebuilt. **Guardrail:** nobody proclaims royal ancestry, a moral selection, or invincibility. Public admiration may tempt Tobious without turning him into a celebrity overnight.

## 10. The Ninth Step
**POV:** Mariya. **Day:** 21. **Target:** 2,600. **Places:** levy yard, Receiver stair, coastal store. **Function:** turn suspicion into usable geography. **Entry:** her first marked message has entered a slow supply chain; she has no answer yet.

1. **Trade an ordinary skill.** Mariya offers to reconcile meal allocations so she can enter the worksite. Darran permits a supervised visit because missing rations threaten productivity. She checks actual recipients against the board, memorizes a discrepancy, and must leave before copying everything. Introduce Thesia, Pehl, and Annet alive through specific requests: a boot repair, a share for a child, a complaint about stale water. Hessah quietly reties a leaking water skin and passes it back, establishing a living person well before her later decline. They are people before they become losses.
2. **Learn a pattern without understanding the machine.** On the ninth stair, water in a cracked bowl trembles before a baffle opens. A guard waits through three clicks before crossing a painted seam. Mariya tries to move during the wrong interval and is sharply pulled back. She revises her observation rather than instantly understanding it. Darran explains only enough to keep his laborers productive. The ancient guards are using repeatable instructions supplied by future contractors.
3. **Build a route outside authority.** At the coastal store, Mariya exchanges a ration tally with a neighboring grain carrier. She learns which path avoids the citadel toll and which household can shelter an injured person. A second marked wrapper records the stair pattern and names. Her goal becomes a reliable contact and escape network, not catching a villain in a confession. Lios is assigned a safe, short delivery within the village.

**Exit:** Mariya knows routes and timings; Darran now knows she counts carefully. **Emotional movement:** suspicion becomes watchful commitment. **Guardrail:** she does not yet know the ultimate purpose of the enclosure. The ninth step is a physical observation point, not a mystical number or final secret switch.

## 11. A Useful Victory
**POV:** Tobious. **Day:** 28. **Target:** 2,800. **Places:** cooling station, maintenance walk, Ypin's kitchen. **Function:** let the new life genuinely reward Tobious before the collapse.

1. **Work under supervision.** A local phase incursion traps a maintenance team between powered doors. Suss directs an intervention with a mapped route and known load; Tobious is assigned one cut and withdrawal. He wants to prove himself but remembers the practice defeat. Ypin identifies the correct seam. Tobious waits for cover, cuts it, and brings out a frightened worker rather than pursuing an Iwang. Success comes from learned procedure and teamwork.
2. **See the attractive shortcut.** Suss demonstrates a brief higher-gain setting after the evacuation, using an isolated artificial test load. It resets more quickly but heats the receiver. He does not pretend the technique has no cost. Tobious sees why emergency operators would want it. Panjios disputes extending it to unmapped living loads; the disagreement is specific, public enough for Tobious to hear, and unresolved.
3. **An evening not devoted to destiny.** Ypin feeds Tobious a meal he thinks is simple; Tobious finds its texture strange and improves it with an ancient preparation. Each admits an ordinary embarrassment. Tobious speaks of Mariya as someone whose judgment he misses, not merely someone he must save. Ypin admits wanting work where a correct repair is enough. Their friendship acquires pleasure, making its later strain matter.

**Exit:** earned confidence, stronger friendship, a credible temptation. **Payoff:** Chapter 8's withdrawal discipline works. **Guardrail:** Suss is not obviously faking the rescue or staging a monster attack; the saved workers remain real witnesses and later matter to Edda.

## 12. What the Records Keep
**POV:** Tobious. **Days:** 35–40, marked transitions. **Target:** 2,900. **Places:** dispatch desk, manifest station, calibration gallery. **Function:** convert homesickness into an evidence-driven return mission.

1. **Receive a voice from home.** Mariya's bundled wrappers arrive through the scheduled physical supply run, delayed by inspections and routing. Her leaf mark authenticates them; household details supply stronger corroboration than a symbol anyone could copy. Tobious wants an immediate crossing. Ypin first compares the names against service records and finds sixty biological loads beneath a protected-person count of zero. The contradiction is visible in two columns, including a familiar name.
2. **Test the first mystery.** Tobious presses Panjios about the opening vision and whether it could reveal how to save people. The diagnostic record shows a forty-one-second acquisition anomaly; no full film survives. Panjios explains the single end-to-inception echo predicted by this damaged reference, with neither selectable date nor controllable replay. Tobious realizes the vision may concern his own future closure, but not when or whether he survives. Do not conceal this deduction from the close POV.
3. **Obtain a limited mission.** Tobious, Ypin, and Panjios present the count discrepancy. Suss authorizes a Day-42 inspection and repair with a narrow safety mandate, retaining remote field command. He needs the reference healthy and the branch productive; an investigation may also protect his operation from uncontrolled failure. Tobious accepts the restrictions to get home, privately intending to intervene if the counts are people. That intention makes the later violation a choice.

**Exit:** a return is scheduled with explicit limits and a meaningful reason Tobious might break them. **Guardrail:** do not treat the entire Concord as knowing a single perfect secret. Ypin has accessed his own manifest layer, not every command file. The message delay predates a working personal relay.

## 13. The First Crossing Back
**POV:** Tobious. **Day:** 42. **Target:** 3,300. **Places:** Salt Gate, Aras yard, Receiver annex. **Function:** midpoint reversal; attempted rescue produces captivity. **Entry:** authorized inspection, not a rescue army.

1. **Return to changed people.** Tobious expects relief; Mariya asks what he has actually learned and what he can promise. They share an unhurried moment of physical affection before urgent work, acknowledging an attachment already present. Samon's brace, empty households, and changed routines make six weeks visible. Ypin gives Mariya a survey wafer that can buffer messages for the service relay; he shows its power limit and diagnostic schedule. This is equipment for inspection, not a miracle gift chosen for a future crisis.
2. **Recognize the human load.** In the annex, the party finds sixty people inside the marked load area, including Mariya, who has come with a food delivery. Darran calls them temporary labor. Tobious sees the restraint geometry tighten when someone tries to leave. Panjios calls for mapping and disengagement before release. Tobious instead opens the visibly occupied branch when a guard threatens a worker; twelve escape into the safe stair. His immediate compassion is real, and his decision ignores a specific redistribution warning.
3. **Pay for incomplete knowledge.** The load shifts into the adjoining zone, tethering the other forty-eight, including Mariya, Lios, Nerissa, and Orran. Iwang crowd the active seams. Panjios prevents Tobious from repeating a cut that would crush the remaining enclosure; Suss orders withdrawal. Tobious retreats with the twelve rescued people and his team through Salt Gate after local villagers take the freed group. Mariya retains the wafer inside. Both see the other remain alive; there is no manufactured belief that she died.

**Exit:** twelve saved; forty-eight trapped; Tobious's homeward triumph is destroyed. **Plant/payoff:** water-route redirection, bounded sword power, physical wafer, moral flaw of unilateral rescue. **Guardrail:** sixty is the total including Mariya, not sixty plus her. None of the forty-eight are transported to the future. Make the redistribution legible in space before describing horror.

## 14. Inside the Hollow
**POV:** Mariya. **Days:** 42–44. **Target:** 2,900. **Places:** annex shelter, water channel, inner baffle. **Function:** establish captivity as a social and logistical conflict with an active viewpoint.

1. **Make a living count.** Mariya counts forty-eight, records injuries, and organizes water collection. Darran's guards deliver rations because live anchors are required; the Iwang are routed by field baffles, not obedient servants. Nerissa rejects Mariya's attempt to do every job. Orran wants the strongest to try an immediate escape; Pehl and Annet argue from different household obligations. Mariya proposes a small test before moving the group.
2. **A test with a loss.** Using the stair pattern, they move a rope through a promising route during a quiet interval. An unstable ledge fails before the return interval; Thesia falls into the active boundary and dies despite an attempted recovery. Do not make her death a punishment for disobedience. The test reveals both a usable service recess and the impossibility of moving tethered people without branch release. Mariya stops a second attempt and gives the reason to everyone.
3. **Send facts instead of reassurance.** Mariya buffers a message: forty-seven alive, one dead, water and ration status, the recess, and the timing pattern. A narrow diagnostic pulse confirms only that the wafer handed the packet to the relay, not that Tobious has read it. She tells the group what is known and still overstates how soon help might come. End on Nerissa asking what happens if the promised interval passes.

**Exit:** forty-seven living captives; a tested limitation and one useful route. **Emotional movement:** shock → purposeful control → guilt at premature certainty. **Guardrail:** Iwang do not imitate Thesia's voice or become her soul. Grief gets human silence, not an invented monster ability.

## 15. The Price of Opening
**POV:** Tobious. **Day:** 44. **Target:** 2,700. **Places:** debrief room, clinic, private offer chamber. **Function:** make responsibility specific without validating Suss's entire policy.

1. **An account he cannot evade.** Tobious demands rescue. Suss reconstructs the failed cut with branch telemetry and twelve successful evacuations beside forty-eight stranded. Tobious initially blames the system, then admits he ignored a warning. Panjios refuses to call the mistake malice but does not absolve it. Ypin retrieves Mariya's packet: forty-seven remain. A death has a name. Tobious cannot honestly repeat 'at least we saved some' as a complete answer.
2. **Look at the cost.** Edda examines field injuries and disputes the protected-person category. She can provide medical observations, not command a full intervention yet. Refugee testimony from the twelve freed people is held by Mariya's local network and can be sought through the supply route; do not magically produce them in her clinic. The future workers saved in Chapter 11 offer directly available corroboration of field effects.
3. **A persuasive smaller mercy.** Suss offers a carefully limited extraction prioritizing Mariya if Tobious supports the anchor program. He argues that known capacity is better than an unplanned rescue, and cites Tobious's actual failure. Tobious does not agree to abandon everyone, but asks how the improved setting works. This keeps temptation active instead of turning him into an instantly perfect dissident. End with Ypin asking whose names are absent from Suss's count.

**Exit:** Tobious is culpable, grieving, and tempted by selective rescue; an alternative needs evidence and capacity. **Guardrail:** Suss's offer must have a real technical route and political exception, not be an obvious lie. Mariya is alive and her wishes have not been consulted.

## 16. The Line Beneath the Seal
**POV:** Tobious. **Day:** 46. **Target:** 2,700. **Places:** maintenance archive, memorial passage, Panjios's workroom threshold. **Function:** expose institutional continuity and break uncomplicated mentor trust.

1. **A bounded search.** Ypin can inspect old maintenance authorizations because the current fault shares an index. He cannot open protected command records. Tobious helps match the evacuation memorial's date to the service log; neither solves it by hacking everything. A sealed route from twelve years earlier carried ninety-seven unregistered people while the official rescue report counted none.
2. **Find a familiar signature.** Panjios authorized the sealing. Suss's command record confirms implementation, and Ypin recognizes a later form he himself has signed. The discovery links present practice to ordinary administrative obedience. Tobious wants to publish immediately; Ypin points out what the evidence proves and what it does not. They retain an authenticated partial copy and identify the missing witness rather than inventing certainty.
3. **Choose confrontation.** Tobious goes to Panjios carrying the discrepancy, not a drawn sword. Panjios sees the old date and does not deny recognizing it. End on the direct question of whether the ninety-seven were alive when he closed the route. The next chapter must answer promptly; do not stretch the cliffhanger into a sequence of evasions.

**Exit:** trust is suspended; Panjios must account for a past choice. **Setup/payoff:** omitted gallery workers, false person-counts, Ypin's permissions, Panjios's protective paternalism. **Guardrail:** the ninety-seven are a separate past incident, never added to the current forty-seven. No recovered diary conveniently explains Suss's entire personality.
