# CHAPTER CARDS 25–32

The climax has distinct objectives: free captives, get them home, part by informed choice, stop the purge, destroy temporal access. Do not merge these into one indistinct battle or postpone every answer to the last chapter.

## 25. The People in the Plan
**POV:** Tobious. **Days:** 69–73. **Target:** 2,900. **Places:** service planning room, future evacuation ward, access checkpoint. **Function:** prove that the new ethic changes practical decisions. **Entry:** a coalition exists but its first plan is inadequate.

1. **Let the plan fail safely.** A scaled walk-through with weights reveals that the initial stretcher sequence leaves two cuts too close together. Tobious proposes taking the load himself; Ypin rejects the bypass. They redesign the order with a holding recess, adult signal backup, and two shield-cover teams. Tobious accepts another person's correction in front of everyone. Record who carries water, who counts, and who can stop a phase cut. Competence feels earned because a plausible first design fails.
2. **Pay the future's share.** Edda moves residents from the most exposed service ward and arranges conventional reservoir and food-tower power. Residents object to losing homes and livelihoods for people they have never seen. Tobious listens, helps with moving rather than making a heroic speech, and admits the city will suffer. Three civic marshals assigned to the emergency review agree to preserve access for inspection crews; they do not become Tobious's private army.
3. **Meet an active opponent.** Suss learns of the civil order and accelerates his purge preparations while restricting ration and technician movements at the ancient receivers. He still needs Tobious's coupling or a forced stabilization around it; an immediate indiscriminate attack would destroy his own objective. The coalition observes the changed routing through Ypin's legitimate maintenance feed. They commit to the rescue window and send the complete proposal to Mariya at the next diagnostic opening.

**Exit:** viable sequence, future civilians included, antagonist responding. **Emotional movement:** resolve → humility under scrutiny → shared commitment. **Guardrail:** the plan should be readable in a physical map and a few decisions, not pages of invented engineering specifications. No unexplained fleet or army appears.

## 26. The Road to Aras
**POV:** Mariya. **Days:** 74–78. **Target:** 2,700. **Places:** captive recess, ration hatch, inner service route. **Function:** let the captives consent and complete the ancient political groundwork.

1. **Receive the whole proposal.** During a logged narrow-band window, Mariya hears the cut sequence, medical limits, Day-84 cutoff, and the permanent closure. She asks whether she is allowed to cross and stay with Tobious. The answer is yes; this is a choice, not an exemption forbidden by the world. She asks time to think while affirming the rescue. Later she tells Nerissa what life in the future could offer and what leaving would cost her personally, beyond being useful to others.
2. **Obtain a physical advantage.** Darran's newly restricted supply run threatens the weak first. Mariya bargains over his recorded meal discrepancy to obtain a supervised water transfer. During it, Orran makes a wax impression of the ordinary baffle latch; local workers can fashion a duplicate. The latch only opens a service panel, not the temporal tether. Lios observes the next ration bell from safety. Every advantage has a previously established scale.
3. **Choose and delegate.** A neighboring carrier smuggles back word that several villages will jointly withhold harbor transport unless Therren grants relief and releases local labor. Mariya gives her network a count and an accountable negotiating demand rather than claiming a foreign god will defeat Telassar. She tells the rescue relay she intends to remain in her own era after release, wants a private farewell if circumstances allow, and will not ask Tobious to leave the root unfinished. Nerissa takes charge of supplies so that Mariya can participate as a person, not an indispensable institution.

**Exit:** consent received, service access prepared, political pressure organized, Mariya's preference known. **Guardrail:** duty cannot be her only identity. Include a concrete desire to live beside this sea, among people who know her history, and help shape a home she chooses. Do not imply staying makes her morally superior to refugees who leave.

## 27. A Gate for Everyone
**POV:** Tobious. **Day:** 82. **Target:** 3,200. **Places:** Salt Gate, village approach, Receiver service recess. **Function:** begin rescue through disciplined action and ancient collective help.

1. **Return without a conqueror's entrance.** Edda's emergency-access crew secures the future Salt interface long enough for Tobious and the trained shield operators Jorra and Sennos to cross. Ypin remains at the future service controls; ordinary radios work locally, while the paired relay handles limited cross-era messages. Therren's men try to stop the party near the village. Tobious blocks and negotiates space rather than slaying bronze-equipped soldiers for spectacle. The captain holds his men back while coastal carriers and his own soldiers argue over a costly, useless confrontation.
2. **Make old competence matter.** Samon, using a brace and cane, directs a work crew moving wedges and stretcher boards through the old drain approach. Tobious recognizes that a broken channel would flood the intended holding recess; they divert it before entry. Shield operators handle threats while the villagers do work outsiders could not infer from a diagram. Tobious's return does not render the ancient world helpless or irrelevant.
3. **Release in the planned order.** The service latch opens; Mariya's team signals the first branch clear. Tobious makes a guarded cut, withstands the exposed interval under another operator's cover, then waits through settling while the released group moves. A threatened shortcut would omit the slowest section. He refuses the bypass and revises the holding order with Mariya's concurrence. End with the first stretchers moving and an Iwang finding a new active seam.

**Exit:** evacuation is physically under way; nobody has been counted safe merely because a tether is cut. **Payoff:** Chapters 1, 8, 10, 23, and 25. **Guardrail:** Tobious is still not Suss's equal in combat. The threat comes from constrained movement and tradeoffs, not every shield conveniently failing at once.

## 28. The Hollow Ones
**POV:** Mariya. **Day:** 83. **Target:** 3,400. **Places:** Receiver annex, braced service passage, outer drainage slope. **Function:** complete rescue with human costs that neither nullify nor glorify it.

1. **Move people, not a number.** Forty-five living captives begin the final passage sequence. Nerissa manages care and roll calls; Lios gives one agreed signal from cover, backed by an adult. Mariya guides the last groups through the wide recess using the baffle cycle. The Iwang flatten only through active thin boundaries and solidify to strike, exposing the collar nexus to covering blades. Show their alien bodily motion and collective pressure; no new voice mimicry or omniscient hunt appears.
2. **Lose people while still trying.** Darran attempts to reassert baffle control to save his commission and remaining route. It exposes him to an Iwang; he dies outside the prisoner count. Orran holds a damaged brace while a stretcher group clears; Tobious and another worker try to relieve him but the structural failure reaches his position first. His choice was informed; the narrative does not declare his life less valuable because he once argued selfishly. Forty-four remain after his death.
3. **Finish an imperfect rescue.** Hessah deteriorates on the final slope. Mariya and Nerissa use the planned rest, water, and available care, but cannot reverse her accumulated injuries. Hessah dies after reaching open air; she is still an evacuation loss, leaving forty-three survivors. Mariya continues the roll until every survivor is matched to a household or carer, including herself. She neither announces total victory nor calls saving forty-three meaningless. End on unfamiliar open sky after weeks underground.

**Exit:** forty-three living survivors safe in the ancient era; no captives remain tethered. **Guardrail:** two evacuation deaths, not an unspecified 'many.' Do not linger on gore, give Hessah a last-minute villainous trait, or use Orran's death as an effortless mechanical solution without attempted relief. The sword's action stays visible from Mariya's limited position.

## 29. What Love Cannot Keep
**POV:** Tobious. **Day:** 83 night–84 before dawn. **Target:** 3,100. **Places:** temporary coastal shelter, path to Salt Gate, threshold. **Function:** pay the romance through informed choice before the physical climax.

1. **An ordinary interval they earned.** After triage and safe shelter assignments, Tobious and Mariya share food and warmth away from the crowd. Forty-three is the living total, including Mariya. She tells him of Pehl, Annet, Thesia, Orran, and Hessah; he tells her what happened to Panjios. They apologize for specific choices without bargaining for forgiveness. Allow restrained physical intimacy consistent with the established relationship; sex is optional, never a required reward or substitute for conversation.
2. **Name the possible lives.** Tobious offers a place with him in Veyr and admits he wants her to choose it. Mariya considers the person she could become there, not merely the duties she would leave. She chooses Aras, where she wants to live and help build a different arrangement with her people. They review the already-known constraints: root cut in the future, less than a day remaining, seven idle days needed for transfer, every receiver sharing the same root. He does not accuse her of loving him less. She does not call his staying a romantic sacrifice owed to her.
3. **A farewell with no fraudulent promise.** At Salt Gate they finish one small familiar act—fastening a repaired wrist binding or sharing the last piece of bread—without inventing a magic keepsake. Jorra and Sennos, their ancient covering duties completed, cross back ahead of Tobious to support the root operation. Tobious crosses to complete the shutdown. Mariya remains with the forty-two other survivors and the wider community. End on him turning once toward a still-open physical threshold; closure has not happened yet. No vow to search for another gate cancels the decision.

**Exit:** both have made and understood the choice; love remains, shared residence will not. **Emotional movement:** relief → tenderness → grief with consent. **Guardrail:** do not repeat the entire technical explanation or make Mariya responsible for preventing every hypothetical future reunion. Their reasons must survive a calm conversation.

## 30. The King's Second Measure
**POV:** Tobious. **Day:** 84, morning. **Target:** 3,300. **Places:** Civic Spine access, purge control junction, Root Hall approach. **Function:** defeat Suss's active plan and resolve Tobious's claim to power through a choice under pressure.

1. **A threat after the hostages.** Suss's ancient-volume purge is armed to preserve the root, even though it will now destroy occupied Aras and neighboring terrain rather than captive anchors alone. The old forty-three do not become magically safe merely by leaving the annex. Edda's civic marshals hold the access order; Suss's loyal operational staff contest it. Some staff stand down on seeing verifiable human counts and Tobious's admission of his part. The institution fractures through evidence and responsibility, not a speech hypnotizing everyone.
2. **Refuse the fair duel.** Suss is a better swordsman. Tobious survives with Ypin's timed cover, terrain knowledge, and shield operators who have trained for this. Suss wounds Tobious in the right ribs during a close exchange. An opening offers a chance to pursue Suss or cut an auxiliary surge route threatening evacuees below. Tobious chooses the route. That choice abandons a plausible revenge opportunity and protects real people while making him temporarily exposed.
3. **Constrain rather than crown.** The disrupted surge isolates Suss from his command link; Edda's marshals and the second operator disarm and detain him. Tobious does not execute him or claim supreme authority. Suss still argues that preserving a city justified his choices; the book need not grant him a repentance speech. The purge is stopped, but accumulated overlap is approaching its measured limit. Ypin takes Tobious to the root seam; only the permanent cut remains.

**Exit:** antagonist detained alive, purge stopped, convergence still active; Tobious has the right-rib wound seen in the opening. **Guardrail:** arrest authority and personnel were established earlier. Three heartbeats and thirty-second settling still apply. A simple arrest cannot repair a damaged lattice.

## 31. The Other Side of His Voice
**POV:** Tobious. **Day:** 84 afternoon; final scene Day 105. **Target:** 3,600. **Places:** Root Hall service lip beneath High Concourse, root seam, rehabilitation ward. **Function:** close the opening's loop, end temporal access, then confirm survival and consequences.

1. **Stand inside the first image.** Root Hall is suspended beneath the upper Civic Spine concourse, with a maintenance lip facing the same skyway seen in Chapter 1. As the overload peaks, the skyway fails despite evacuation; people fall. Do not later dismiss the vision as fake or claim everyone had a hidden harness. Later reports confirm three deaths here, separate from the captive losses. Tobious, shorter-haired, scarred beneath the left eye and bleeding at his right ribs, recognizes his younger face on the acquisition echo. Repeat the patched Chapter-1 speech exactly, with the original order and interruptions; no second prophecy or extra warning added at will. Forty-one seconds expire.
2. **Remain through the cost.** Ypin and the operators keep a physical approach clear while Tobious places Severance against the root seam. The terminal cut needs fifteen continuous heartbeats of living corrective contact. Every beat has a bodily and spatial consequence; do not fill the passage with a catalogue of philosophical quotations. Tobious has a chance to let go when the load damages his right forearm, but completing the agreed task protects both occupied volumes. The reference mineral and blade core are consumed. All three temporal receiver accesses fail permanently. Ordinary independently powered shields continue to function. Tobious falls injured into reachable space; Ypin gets him to medical care.
3. **Live after the heroic moment.** Three weeks later, Veyr has rationing, displaced residents, repaired temporary bridges, and a public inquiry. Panjios's full record, including the ninety-seven, is preserved alongside his final act; Suss is detained pending proceedings, not secretly escaped. Tobious has lasting right-hand tremor and reduced strength. With rehabilitation guidance, he chooses a modest useful task beside Ypin, adapting how he works. He grieves Mariya without treating continued life as betrayal. Severance's inert remains are evidence and memory, never a glowing promise of another gate.

**Exit:** Tobious unmistakably alive in the future, permanently separated; institutional reckoning begun, not instant utopia. **Guardrail:** no return message through a relic, dream with verified new information, reincarnation, clone, or future archive that gives a complete reassuring account of Mariya's life. Her ending belongs to her viewpoint.

## 32. What Is Kept
**POV:** Mariya. **Day:** 112, first autumn rains after the long occupied summer. **Target:** 2,600. **Places:** Aras meeting yard, rebuilt watercourse, barley terrace. **Function:** resolve the ancient political and emotional story through a lived future.

1. **A limited political victory.** The carrier coalition, loss of the secret receiver commission, and the cost of holding the damaged valley have forced Therren to accept seasonal levy relief, release remaining ordinary detainees, and withdraw the village garrison to the ridge. Telassar still exists and the charter is imperfect. Mariya shares verification with household representatives instead of privately assuring everyone it is safe. Mention who watches compliance. This completes work established in Chapters 7, 10, and 26.
2. **Remember without freezing.** The forty-three surviving captives are placed within the wider village, not mistaken for its entire population. Nerissa resumes ordinary care, Samon teaches repairs with a lasting limp, Lios has work and play protected from exploitation. Five captive losses are named in a communal record together with the earlier occupation dead as separate entries. Mariya does not know Tobious's medical outcome by magic; she knows he crossed alive and chose a survivable task, and chooses to live without certainty. The reader, having Chapter 31, knows both survived.
3. **Plant what was preserved.** One of the two protected seed jars has been spent feeding people; the last is opened for communal sowing alongside grain obtained through neighbors. The original leaf-marked family jar was cracked and its grain seized: use the agreed leaf mark on a new tag, not a miraculously restored relic. Mariya joins the work, allows another person to decide a row's spacing, and feels Tobious's absence within ordinary sensation. End on planting under rain, carrying grief and future desire together. No monster stinger. No promise that separation will be undone.

**Exit:** community incomplete but living; Mariya able to love, grieve, decide, and continue. **Payoff:** jars, measures, water, names, shared work, consent. **Guardrail:** survival is not a consolation prize or punishment for choosing home. Do not state she will never love anyone else. Do not summarize her entire lifetime.
