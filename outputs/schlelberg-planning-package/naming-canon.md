## Use in prose

Use Tobious, Panjios, and Lios as the stable narration/record forms. Tobi, Panji, and Lio are familiar names in dialogue when the relationship permits them. Establish each full-name/familiar-name connection naturally at introduction; do not insert a pronunciation lecture into the scene. Mariya may call Tobious Tobi privately; she gives his full name when identifying him to an official. Panjios can invite his trainees to call him Panji. Lios can identify himself and add that people call him Lio. Avoid turning every switch into a symbolic event or implying that a short name is a separate person.

The opening's future self addresses his younger self as **“Tobi. Listen. They aren't looking for stores.”** Chapter 31 must repeat the exact same form. This naming update does not apply the separate pending change to the opening's final warning.

**Ypin is i-pin**, not ai-pin. A single unobtrusive cast/pronunciation guide in a future edition is possible if needed; no change of spelling is authorized merely because a reader might initially pronounce it differently. Thesia, Nerissa, Sennos, Samon, and the longer formal names intentionally differ somewhat from the old pronunciations because the author selected those options.

All other names in the table have no newly invented nickname. Use Suss, never the old Sus spelling in current-story prose. Use Mariya, never Maria in current-story prose. The Iwang / the Hollow Ones remains unchanged: it is a species label, not part of this character rename. Locations, organizations, sword names, chronology, and the book title remain unchanged.

The original DOCX, original editorial request, factual original-manuscript map, and archived earlier snapshots retain their original names. Source-analysis passages may quote those names explicitly. Use this mapping to interpret them; never mistake an original spelling for a new cast member. Only naming and its documentation changed in revision 1.1. The 32 chapters, 96 scene cards, POV allocation, captive counts, and permanently separated surviving couple are unchanged. All six naming batches are complete; do not ask the author to choose again.
